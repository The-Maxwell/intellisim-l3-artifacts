## Rule

- A claim is **TRUE** when the computed quantity matches the report's figure within rounding (±1 agent or ±0.15 agents/step), **PARTLY** when the qualitative statement holds but a stated number is off (or the reverse), **FALSE** when the series contradict it, **UNCHECK** when the claim is a mechanism or attribution that no exported quantity decides.
- A claimed *regime change / surge / slowdown* between two windows counts as supported when the later window's mean per-step rate differs from the earlier one by ≥ 30 % (`REGIME_FACTOR`).
- A claimed *secondary wave* in an SIR series counts as supported when, after the main peak, I(t) falls to a trough and later rises to ≥ 3× the trough and ≥ 5 infected (`REBOUND_FACTOR`, `REBOUND_MIN`).
- Per-step exits = Δ`evacuated_count`; «steps a–b» is inclusive; the EXP-C/E runs record steps 0…T where `evacuated_count` reaches the population at step T.
- Columns *pass 1 / pass 2* say how each scoring pass treated the statement: `error`, `halluc`, `note` (mentioned, not counted), `unknown` (declared not checkable), `score k` (drove a phenomenon score), `none` (not mentioned).

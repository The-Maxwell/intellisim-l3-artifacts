**Phenomenon points: 4/6**

Factual errors (verbatim + what the data says): count = 1
- "The simulation ends at step 98 with one straggler still inside" (repeated as "Simulation completes at step 98 with one straggler remaining."). The data say every agent exits and the run terminates condition_met (population → 0) at step 98, so no agent is left inside.

Hallucinated phenomena (verbatim + why unsupported): count = 2
- "Stop-and-go wave propagation emerging from collision rules and crowd-following decisions." (also "producing bottleneck congestion and stop-and-go waves"). This is asserted as an emergent phenomenon, but the only evidence cited is aggregate population counts and two snapshots. Nothing in the data supports a propagating wave.
- "Low rates of “seek_alternate_route” and “wait_briefly” decisions prevent effective decongestion and route diversification." (also "Sparse use of seek_alternate_route and wait_briefly → insufficient decongestion → oscillatory evacuation bursts."). The room has a single door. The data show the LLM-decision run is slower than the rules-only reference (98 vs 46 steps), so nothing supports the claim that more alternate-route or wait decisions would decongest egress.

Actionable recommendations: y - quote: "Widen the exit or add additional doors in the model to assess effects on throughput and tail-phase duration."
